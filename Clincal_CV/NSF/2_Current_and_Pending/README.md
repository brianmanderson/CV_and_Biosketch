## SciENcv click path

1. Go to <https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sciencv/> and sign in with eRA Commons (`BrianMAnderson`) or ORCID (`0000-0002-2748-1444`). Both land in the same NCBI account once linked.
2. From My NCBI choose **Manage SciENcv → Create New Document**.
3. Enter a document name (for example `NSF Current and Pending 2026`), choose format **NSF Current and Pending (Other) Support** (NIH Biosketch is the default, so change it), and **Start with a blank document**.
4. **Section 1 – Identifying information, organization and location:** paste the values from the worksheet's Section 1 table.
5. **Section 2 – Projects and proposals:** click **Add** and paste Entry 1 (this NSF proposal, Pending) field by field. Person-months go in per budget year, current period first; SciENcv's "Year" field takes the later calendar year of a budget period that spans two years. A zero person-months value is rejected: if effort on a project is zero, leave the project out. Add a further block only for support Brian confirms.
6. **Section 3 – In-kind contributions:** leave empty unless something in the worksheet's in-kind section turns out to apply.
7. **View Draft** to check the PDF at any time.
8. Tick the **certification** yourself (see below), then **Download PDF**. Save it as `NSF/2_Current_and_Pending/Anderson_BM_NSF_CurrentPending.pdf`.
9. Upload the PDF under Senior Personnel Documents in Research.gov (or as a Grants.gov attachment). SciENcv does not push to NSF systems.
10. Expect to regenerate it: NSF requires updated Current and Pending information from every senior/key person before it makes a funding recommendation, and again at any point during the award on request.

## Certification cannot be delegated

- A delegate (research administrator, assistant) may draft and edit the document, but **only Brian can certify it**, from his own SciENcv login. A delegate who clicks Download PDF on an uncertified document gets "Certification Required" and can only send Brian a notification ([NSF SciENcv FAQ Q10](https://www.nsf.gov/policies/document/faq-using-sciencv)).
- The certification states the information is current, accurate, and complete, covering foreign and domestic support, and that he is not a party to a malign foreign talent recruitment program. NSF does not accept a digital signature in place of the SciENcv certification (FAQ Q12).
- The downloaded PDF may be renamed but must not be exported, opened, and re-saved in another application (FAQ Q11). The document must be the 2024-1 version, which SciENcv produces automatically (FAQ Q7).
