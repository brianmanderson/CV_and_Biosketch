2NSF Current & Pending (Other) Support (SciENcv)

PAPPG 24-1 II.D.2.h(ii). Also prepared in SciENcv, no page limit. NSF asks for an updated version before any award is made.

What must be disclosed

  • Every proposal and active project that supports your research, from any source: federal, state, foreign, foundation, industry, or internal institutional funds. Include this NSF proposal itself as Pending.
  • In-kind contributions from outside your organization worth $5,000 or more that require your time (lab space, equipment, datasets, personnel). Under $5,000, or with no time commitment, need not be listed.
  • Consulting that involves research, could affect your commitments, or is under a contract requiring you to conceal the relationship. The dollar amount you receive goes in the total field.
  • Closed awards such as the 2019 SIR Allied Scientist Grant and the 2020 to 2021 Kopchick Fellowship are not listed.

Handling notes

  • Rows need not be sorted. The Last Active column is optional; leave it blank for ongoing relationships and estimate otherwise.
  • Insert rows by selecting row numbers inside a table, right-click, Insert. Do not rename sheets, delete headers, or change column order.
  • Supervised master's students who actually collaborated on research go in Table 4 as C, not in Table 3.
  • Recompute the 48- and 24-month windows from the real submission date before uploading.

What counts

Innovations in teaching and training, contributions to the science of learning, research tools and databases made available to others, broadening participation in STEM, international collaboration, standards development, and service to the scientific community. Pitfalls

  • A biosketch or current-and-pending PDF made in Word instead of SciENcv is non-compliant; the system rejects it.
  • Certification cannot be done by a delegate or the sponsored-projects office. Sign in yourself.
  • Keep names and dates identical across all four documents and the CV (for example "University of California, San Diego" everywhere).
  • Synergistic activities inside the biosketch, or more than one page or five items, are both grounds for return.
  • The COA must keep NSF's layout. Do not export it from Google Sheets or Numbers; save from Excel or openpyxl as .xlsx.
  • Record the submission date on the COA and recompute the 48-month and 24-month windows if the deadline moves.

Repo hygiene. Final PDFs and the .xlsx live under Clincal_CV/NSF/ beside the drafts. Commit them together with this guide so the repo never holds a half-updated set. The CV and NIH biosketch are untouched by this work.
